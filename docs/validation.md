## What this does and does not show

- It shows that PEDRO's algebra reproduces VEDA's tables on identical input, that it reproduces published PED values to the extent the underlying calculation does, and that it recomputes each program's frequencies from its Hessian.
- It does not validate any structure, coordinate choice or band assignment. Those remain scientific judgments; PEDRO reports the evidence (forces, imaginary modes, coordinate set) needed to make them.
